# ❤️ About CookeryBookery

CookeryBookery is Ammu & Achu's recipe collection — because deciding what to eat shouldn't take longer than cooking it!

🍅 Browse by meal, 🌍 cuisine or 😋 taste. Each recipe retains a link to the original saved YouTube video.

This is a personal draft catalogue. Video titles and links come from an archived playlist. Tags are provisional; ingredients and instructions will be added after video verification.
